Signal is the household display and messaging app for this home. It is provided for family use, not as a commercial service.

Who may use it

Household members use an invited account. Guests may use the public pages the house has turned on (games, guest snaps, guest book) and should keep that use decent and short-lived.

The house stays in charge

A household admin can add or remove accounts, change what is on the board, and turn features off. If you have a login, it is for this household only — do not share it outside the family.

What you post

Photos, guest-book notes, and other messages you send are meant for this house. Do not post anything you would not want on the kitchen wall. The house may remove anything at any time.

Email

If Gmail is linked, Signal may send password-reset and invite mail from that household address. That link can be removed in Admin.

Signal is a home project. It is offered as-is. Displays can be late, mail can fail, and the server can be down. Use it knowing that.
