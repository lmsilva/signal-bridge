Signal

Privacy policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

Signal is a private household app. It runs on our home server and is meant for family and invited guests — not as a public product.

Who this covers

This page is for people who sign in as a household member, guests who leave a photo or a guest-book note, and Google (so we can send a few house emails).

What we keep

  • Household usernames, names, optional email addresses, and avatars.
  • Guest-book notes and Guest Snaps photos that someone chooses to send to the house displays.
  • Settings and activity for the house board and overlays (timers, music, games, and similar).
  • A Gmail send token if an admin links a household Gmail account. We ask Google only for gmail.send. We do not read your inbox, contacts, or other mail.

How we use it

We use this information to run the house: sign-in, password resets, guest invites, and putting things on the displays. We do not sell it, show ads, or use it to train public models.

Who else sees it

Data stays on the household server except when a feature you turned on needs an outside service (for example Google, to send mail). Those services only get what that feature needs.

How long we keep it

Account and board data stay until a household admin changes or deletes them. Guest photos and notes stay until someone in the house removes them. Unlinking Gmail deletes the stored send token.

Questions

Talk to the household admin. This is a family service, not a company help desk.
